You may use the Marks without prior written permission in the following cases:

**2.1 Descriptive / Nominative Use**

You may use the word "StemJSON" to truthfully describe your product's
relationship to the StemJSON specification, provided:

- The use is purely descriptive and factually accurate
- Your product name does not begin with, or consist of a variant
  confusingly similar to, "StemJSON" (examples of variants the Owner
  treats as confusingly similar: "Stem-JSON", "StemJson", "Stemson",
  "StemJSN")
- The reference is clearly subordinate to your own brand

Permitted examples:

- "Acme App — built with StemJSON"
- "Our server delivers StemJSON payloads"
- "This library parses StemJSON files"
- "A talk about StemJSON at SwiftConf 2026"
- "StemJSON tutorial" (in a blog post title)

**2.2 Community and Educational Use**

- Blog posts, articles, tutorials, and documentation about StemJSON
- Conference talks and meetup presentations
- Open-source tools and libraries that work with StemJSON (provided they
  follow the naming rules in Section 3)
- Academic papers and research

The Owner reserves the right to establish a formal StemJSON Certification

"StemJSON Certified" mark exists and the phrase must not be used.**

If and when a program is launched, certified implementations will be
entitled to use the "StemJSON Certified" mark under a separate
certification agreement, which is expected to include:

- Passing the official StemJSON conformance test suite
- Maintaining compatibility with the current stable specification version

Details will be published at https://stemjson.com/certification when
available.


## 6. Attribution Requirements

### 6.1 Required Attribution for Implementations

Any product, library, or service that implements the StemJSON specification
MUST include the following attribution in a reasonably discoverable location
(e.g., documentation, about screen, README, license file, or third-party
notices):

    "StemJSON specification created by Vasyl Krychun
     https://stemjson.com"

This requirement is part of the specification license (see LICENSE). It
applies to all implementations regardless of platform, language, or
commercial status.

### 6.2 Style Guidelines

When referencing StemJSON in documentation or marketing materials:

- First mention: "StemJSON" (capitalise both S and JSON)
- Preferred usage is adjectival — e.g., "a StemJSON payload", "the
  StemJSON specification", "a StemJSON-compatible editor". Avoid using
  the mark as a verb (e.g., "to StemJSON something").
- Include the following notice where practical:
  "StemJSON is a trademark of Vasyl Krychun."
